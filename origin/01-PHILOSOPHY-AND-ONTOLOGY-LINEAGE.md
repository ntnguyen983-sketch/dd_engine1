# DUYÊN DỊCH — PHILOSOPHY & ONTOLOGY LINEAGE

## 1. Earliest computational-philosophical anchor

The earliest preserved technical lineage already refuses to treat a thing as a fixed essence. v2.3.6 states that observed things are components of a snapshot in the flow, not fixed entities, and that the model concerns the movement of the flow. It also establishes non-determinism, dynamic impermanence, recursive non-self, absolute traceability and conditional validity. fileciteturn36file1

The important point for the Origin is not to preserve every historical label as eternal truth. The important invariant is the direction of reasoning: observation → relation/condition → measured dynamics → conditional description.

## 2. Dao–Duyên–Dịch as system language

Historical definitions describe:

- **Đạo** as the lawful operation of dependent arising and impermanence.
- **Duyên** as a temporarily stable pattern emerging from a network of conditions.
- **Dịch** as transformation, evolution or disappearance of such patterns as conditions change.

These definitions are explicitly conditional and dynamic. fileciteturn36file2

For the current anchor, these words should not be treated as additional machine labels that assert a permanent essence. They describe the conceptual direction of the model.

## 3. Quẻ as snapshot

The lineage repeatedly constrains the quẻ: it is a snapshot/state-positioning device, not a deterministic cause of an event. v2.3.7 states the kernel proposition that the quẻ positions a state at the Thiên–Địa–Nhân intersection and the system reads a tendency of movement in the flow. fileciteturn34file3

The v2.8.7 Rev.B lineage retains the same rule: the quẻ is a space-time snapshot at observation/initialization `t0`, not a single deterministic cause. fileciteturn34file9

## 4. Seven-layer conceptual progression

The v2.3.7 architecture is useful as historical lineage:

`Universe → Coordinate → Object → Relation → Dynamic Engine → Evaluation → Interpretation`.

Its five organizing rules are effectively:

1. establish a reference frame;
2. locate the object/actor;
3. establish relation;
4. calculate dynamics;
5. evaluate before interpreting.

fileciteturn34file3

The final anchor should retain the dependency order without requiring the historical layer names to be permanent ontological categories.

## 5. A0–A12 / UK1–UK8 lineage

The early axiomatic layer contains principles such as binary primitive state, bounded existence, energy decay, semantic neutrality of Qi, full traceability, empirical treatment of hyperparameters, kernel-only state transitions, observer interaction, reconstruction uncertainty, model separation, actor localization, interaction and phase reference. fileciteturn36file6

UK1–UK8 extend this into a universal encoding policy: the kernel should not process traditional semantics directly; knowledge maps into primitives; different knowledge systems can act as observers; uncertainty is exposed; traceability is bidirectional; fusion uses reliability; decoders translate rather than mutate the simulation; adapters extend the system without modifying the kernel. fileciteturn34file7

These are historical architectural commitments. Where later versions simplified or reframed them, both the original and the resolution must remain visible in the Origin.

## 6. The current abstraction

The latest architectural anchor deliberately removes permanent evaluative labels. The machine-facing expressive basis is:

### Định danh
A reference token used to distinguish observation streams, entities, spaces, events or relations. It is not proof of a permanent self or essence.

### Định lượng
Measured or explicitly supplied quantities: time, position, magnitude, flow, interval, repetition, uncertainty, spatial node, etc.

### Định tính
Observed pattern derived from the quantitative/evidence layer: convergence, divergence, recurrence, rhythm, irregularity, transition and other descriptions. It must remain traceable to quantities and evidence and must not silently become a moral or ontological judgment.

Thus the architectural movement is not “old theory versus new theory”. It is a progressive reduction of assumptions: preserve what can be referenced, measured and conditionally described; keep everything else as interpretation or historical context.
