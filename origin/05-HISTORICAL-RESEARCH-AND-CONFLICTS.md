# DUYÊN DỊCH — HISTORICAL RESEARCH, EXPERIMENTS & CONFLICTS

This document is intentionally a quarantine layer. Historical ideas are preserved because they explain how the system evolved, but they are not automatically part of the current anchor.

## v2.3.9: explicit empirical research separation

v2.3.9 explicitly says the Engine is a hypothesis and that real-world logs determine validity; it separates a Frozen Core Kernel from a Research Pack containing hypotheses, open variables, parameter tuning, validation logs and solver plugins. fileciteturn42file0

Its hypothesis-testing layer records ACCEPT / REJECT / INCONCLUSIVE rather than forcing a conclusion when data are insufficient. fileciteturn42file1

This is historically valuable and should remain a core methodological lesson: lack of evidence is a legitimate state.

## v2.5.x: expanded physics/simulation proposals

v2.5.3 introduced a much more ambitious 12-layer simulation formulation, including configuration potential, force gradient, potential surface, adjacency, differential evolution, entropy/lifecycle evaluation, stochastic prediction, Bellman strategy and feedback calibration. fileciteturn42file12 fileciteturn42file13

These formulations are archived as research history. They must not be silently merged into the current canonical architecture unless a later source explicitly re-adopts them and closes their contracts.

## Feedback-loop conflict

Some historical research versions proposed automatic feedback/self-correction that writes updated weights back into earlier computational layers. For example, v2.5.1 describes Layer 12 feeding parameter updates back to Layers 3, 4 and 7. fileciteturn41file15

Later frozen-core architecture explicitly forbids Observation/Warning/Recommendation feedback from mutating Core. Therefore the historical feedback loop is preserved as research lineage but is not part of the current frozen runtime contract.

## Semantic-label conflict

Earlier versions contain named qualitative states such as Dưỡng, Hy, Ân, Nhiễu, Tà and Sát, and some historical interpretation layers map these to strategies. fileciteturn41file13

The current architectural anchor removes permanent evaluative labeling as ontology. Qualitative output remains an observed pattern derived from quantities/evidence. Historical state labels therefore belong in the archive/decoder layer, not as immutable substance.

## Physics-claim boundary

v2.5.3 itself warns that its “foundational physics” are modeling axioms, not experimentally verified natural laws. fileciteturn42file13

The Origin must preserve this distinction. Duyên Dịch may use mathematical analogies and model assumptions, but an internal equation is not thereby a discovered law of nature.

## Formula conflicts

The v2.9.2 merged source explicitly records unresolved verification items including elemental mapping, MChi_Rel conditions, scalar-to-vector force contract, priority ordering, F_norm/calibration and DPKE mapping. fileciteturn36file0

These are not defects to hide. They are provenance markers. A production implementation must use only a verified canonical mapping or require the missing contract.

## Origin rule for contradictions

When two historical versions disagree:

1. preserve both source statements;
2. identify the exact conflict;
3. record the later resolution if one exists;
4. otherwise mark the issue unresolved;
5. never invent a bridge formula merely to make the documents look consistent;
6. implementation branches may choose only a declared, source-backed contract.

This is the mechanism that allows the Origin to be complete without pretending the history was linear or perfectly consistent.
