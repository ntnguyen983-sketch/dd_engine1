# v2.3.6 — Kernel, ontology and 7-layer lineage

## Status in lineage

Historical source. Preserve as lineage; do not treat every historical label as a current v3.4 ontology rule.

## Core proposition

The v2.3.6 kernel establishes the key separation: quẻ does not "predict an event" as an independent cause. It positions a state/snapshot, and the analysis concerns the observed Actor/flow at the Thiên–Địa–Nhân intersection.

## Architecture freeze

The v2.3.6 freeze policy locks Constitution, Axioms, Ontology, PGL/OCG, layered architecture, Pipeline, Firewall and Traceability. Runtime/Tick Engine, Cost Functions, Adapters, Plugins, APIs and SDKs remain extensible. Changes are separated into bug fixes, extensions and architectural changes.

## Ontology and axioms carried forward

- No Duyên exists completely independently; interaction follows co-presence of conditions.
- Interaction of background conditions generates system-level field/force/orientation.
- Force, direction and potential determine network transformation.
- Continuous movement of the network is the Dòng.
- Quẻ is a snapshot of the Dòng at observation time.
- Forecasting evaluates possible transformation tendencies, not a predetermined outcome.

The mathematical layer also introduced bounded existence, energy/momentum decay in isolated conditions, semantic independence of the field vector, and full traceability from output interpretation back to source state/operators.

## Universal Knowledge Encoding

UK1–UK8 establish semantic independence, primitive completeness, observer equivalence, information preservation, bidirectional traceability, fusion neutrality, decoder isolation and extensibility. A knowledge tradition is therefore an observer/adapter, not an authority that may mutate the computational kernel.

## 7-layer runtime

`Universe → Coordinate → Object → Relation → Dynamic Engine → Evaluation → Interpretation`

The organizational order is intentional: reference frame first, positioning second, relation third, dynamics fourth, evaluation after dynamics, interpretation after evaluation.
