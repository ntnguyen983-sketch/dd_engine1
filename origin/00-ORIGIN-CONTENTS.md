## Organization rule

- `origin/philosophy/` — conceptual lineage and principles.
- `origin/specs/` — versioned technical specifications and reconciliations.
- `origin/architecture/` — current architectural anchors and boundaries.
- `origin/ontology/` — N(n), Observation, Interaction, Space, Time, State and the ID/Quantity/Quality expression model.
- `origin/dynamics/` — force/flow, bottleneck, rhythm, transition and calibration concepts.
- `origin/validation/` — gates, invariants, provenance, ground truth and test obligations.
- `origin/adapters/` — rules for converting domain inputs such as quẻ/hào into the general ontology; adapters do not redefine the origin.
- `origin/implementation-boundary/` — what implementations may change without changing the architectural anchor.

## Authority rule

The origin archive is descriptive and historical. A later implementation may be optimized, rewritten or replaced. An implementation must not manufacture formulas, labels or state values and call them canonical when the origin does not define them. Where a value requires L4 calibration or Ground Truth and is unavailable, it remains unknown/None rather than being guessed.
